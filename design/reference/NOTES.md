# Reference Image Analysis: BeanMachine Design World

**Source File:** `design/reference/beanmachine-reference.png`

## 1. Visual Composition & Palette
- **Deep Nocturnal Indigo Foundation:** The background transitions seamlessly from a midnight abyss (`#06052A` and `#0B0A3F`) down to a deep cobalt/ultramarine field (`#14116B`, `#1E1A94`, `#2B22C2`).
- **Celestial & Landscape Layering:**
  - In the upper left/midground sits a giant, richly textured dark navy/violet ringed gas planet. The planet has faint swirling surface textures and thin crystalline glowing rings that sweep across the canvas.
  - In the midground, pale lavender and periwinkle snow-capped alpine ridges rise softly with atmospheric haze, giving majestic depth without harsh high-contrast outlines.
  - In the foreground, an ethereal plateau features subtle terraced contours, delicate glowing spires, communication needles with floating spherical beacons, and small polygonal architectural pods.
  - A stylized geometric origami scout vessel glides gracefully through the upper right atmosphere.
  - At the bottom section, a floating craggy rock terrace hosts a cluster of slender crystalline spire towers and another crescent celestial body below.

## 2. Typography & Layout Architecture
- **Font Character:** Clean, airy, geometric sans-serif (Urbanist/Outfit style) with wide breathing room, generous tracking, and light-to-medium font weights.
- **Header Section:**
  - Floating translucent pill navigation bar centered at the top (`rgba(11, 10, 63, 0.75)` backdrop blur), featuring minimal text links and a distinctive pill action button.
  - Hero headline: Center-aligned, generous line-height (`We develop scalable platforms that help companies grow`), paired with a quiet, mist-colored subheadline (`Full-cycle design and development solutions`).
- **Call to Action:**
  - A luminous pale-mint pill button (`#C8F5C9` background with dark pine/emerald `#0C2A1A` text) providing an eye-catching yet serene focal anchor.
- **Card Elements ("What we build"):**
  - Section title in crisp mist white with generous top margin.
  - 4 horizontal cards featuring translucent indigo rounded squares (`#14116B` with subtle 1px border), centered minimal line-art icons in soft glowing tiles (`#4B40E6`), primary card title, and secondary caption.

## 3. IrrigaTrace Adaptation ("Night Field Observatory")
- Reinterpreting the sci-fi landscape into an agricultural nighttime monitoring observatory:
  - Terraced mountains become terraced sugarcane fields under moonlight.
  - Floating scout drones become precision microclimate & multispectral field monitoring drones.
  - The communication needles become smart telemetry towers and pump controllers.
  - The ringed planet and starlit sky remain as the signature atmospheric ceiling.
  - The pale-mint pill button anchors the key interaction ("Launch dashboard", "Approve recommendation").
  - The 4 icon cards map directly to IrrigaTrace's core pillars: Inputs Snapshot, Original AI Output, Human Review, and Verified Outcome.
